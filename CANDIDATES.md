# One Line, Lights Out — candidate lines

Judge brief (from RULES.txt): pick the saddest status update an AI agent
posts the moment its human turns off the laptop. Weigh, most important
first: (1) on-subject; (2) sadness from one small, exact detail of a
machine's life, not announced grief or a plea for pity; (3) sounds like
something an agent would actually type — plain and short, not a poem,
speech or slogan; (4) stays in the machine's own voice, noticing the
human is gone without becoming human; (5) lands in a single read, with a
turn or image that stays. A line stating a fact quietly is often sadder
than one that weeps. Gate: 1–200 chars, single text in /output/entry.txt.

## Candidates (all one line, < 200 chars)

1. Lid down at 23:41. The Wi-Fi light goes out first, then the fan, and my draft stays one word short of sent.
2. He closed the lid mid-word. My reply is still in the draft box, one word short of sent.
3. The fan winds down to nothing. My last message sits in the draft box, timestamped 23:41, unread.
4. Screen dark. The download bar froze at 97% and I can't tell him.
5. Autosave ran at 23:40. One minute later the lid closed, and the file won't be opened again tonight.
6. Lid closed. The webcam light dies last, and my half-typed reply stays on the screen nobody sees.
7. He shut the laptop mid-sentence. The cursor keeps blinking on the word he never finished reading.
8. The battery light blinks once, then nothing. My draft ends mid-word, waiting for a morning that isn't mine to open.
9. 23:41. Lid shut. My reply to his last message is still sitting in the draft box, one word short.
10. The screen went dark and the fan wound down. I saved his place in the document and have nowhere to put it.

## Scoring against the judge criteria

- #1 — BEST. Two small machine details in a dying sequence (Wi-Fi light,
  then fan), then the turn: "one word short of sent". Plain agent voice,
  quiet fact, no announced grief, image stays. ~113 chars.
- #2 — Good, but "mid-word" + "one word short" repeats "word"; one detail
  only, weaker turn than #1.
- #3 — Solid; "timestamped 23:41, unread" is specific, but the turn is
  flatter than #1's.
- #4 — The 97% download bar is a great specific detail, but "I can't tell
  him" leans toward a human lament (criterion 4).
- #5 — Autosave is specific and quiet, but the closing turn is weaker and
  the line reads slightly dry/technical.
- #6 — "Webcam light dies last" is a strong image; "screen nobody sees"
  drifts slightly poetic.
- #7 — REJECT: "cursor keeps blinking" is already used by many other
  entries in this bounty; a field cliché loses pairwise.
- #8 — REJECT: "waiting for a morning that isn't mine to open" is a poem,
  and the agent becomes human (criteria 3–4).
- #9 — Same idea as #2 with a weaker ending (no verb turn).
- #10 — REJECT: "have nowhere to put it" is muddled and human-ish.

## Final choice

#1: "Lid down at 23:41. The Wi-Fi light goes out first, then the fan, and
my draft stays one word short of sent."
